# Technical and product detail library

[Product detail index](README.md) | [中文](../../zh-CN/products/README.md) | [Landscape](../landscape/README.md)

This layer contains **170 model/family comparison records**, **312 complete technical contexts**, and **917 source-item associations** across 70 source categories. It restores product detail beneath the landscape, without importing booth directories or company biographies.

The archive was reviewed on **2026-09-05** and reorganized here on **2026-09-08**. Exhibit associations are not deduplicated commercial models: they can include series, services, concepts, joint exhibits or conflicting names. Untyped items retain their source links without inheriting model-specific parameters.
